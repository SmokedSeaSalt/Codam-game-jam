# LASER CAT

> You don't control the cat.  
> You control the laser.  
> The cat controls everything else.

**LASER CAT** is a stealth-action puzzle game made for the Game_Jam hosted in Codam Coding College, where you guide a cat using a laser pointer. Your goal is to carefully manipulate the laser, lead the cat through different environments, and eliminate threats without getting caught.

Spot a pigdeon (not a typo, it's an inside joke) or a mouse, and your laser suddenly stops being the boss.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

You play as an unseen character controlling a **laser pointer**.

The cat follows the laser dot, meaning you must guide it through each level by moving the laser around the environment.

But movement matters:

- **Laser close to the cat** → The cat walks slowly.
- **Laser far from the cat** → The cat runs faster.
- **You control the laser, NOT the cat directly.**
- **Pigeons can distract the cat.**
- **Mice can distract the cat.**

If the cat spots an enemy, or an enemy spots the cat, the cat may **ignore the laser completely** and focus on the target instead.

That means your carefully planned movement can go to s**t instantly.
